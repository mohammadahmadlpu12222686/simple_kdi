# Supplier notice: delivery delay from Apex Motors

Date: 3 October 2026. Received by email from Apex Motors.

Apex Motors informs us that all Motor deliveries will be late by 14 days. The reason for the delay is a strike at the port.

Apex Motors expects normal delivery lead time to return after the strike ends. Until then, every open Motor order from Apex Motors will arrive 14 days later than planned.

Apex Motors is our primary supplier for the Motor.
