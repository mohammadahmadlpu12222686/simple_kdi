# Supplier onboarding: Delta Windings

Date: 8 October 2026. Prepared by the purchasing team.

Delta Windings is approved as a new supplier. Delta Windings will supply the component Motor.

During the audit we learned how Delta Windings gets its materials. Delta Windings depends on the sub-supplier Kolar Metals. Kolar Metals supplies the raw material Copper.

The component Motor requires the raw material Copper. A shortage of copper would therefore slow down every motor we buy.
